Made for a first step

Start small. Keep control.

Touch is an entry-level device for people trying self-custody for the first time. The aim is to make a safer way to explore crypto feel approachable, without turning the wallet into a disposable demo.

01 / ACCESSIBLE

€15 price point

A more approachable starting point for a dedicated hardware wallet.

02 / SIMPLE

Connect and explore

Get from the sealed pouch to your first wallet experience with a short setup path.

03 / YOUR KEYS

Offline by design

Private keys stay on the device while the connected computer or phone handles the wider experience.

What comes with Touch

Unseal. Connect. Begin.

Touch is distributed in a branded, sealed foil pouch rather than a retail box. Inside is the essentials-only kit for getting started.

Full capability, accessible price

Entry-level does not mean stripped down.

Touch runs the full Nucleo OS foundation and includes the protected functions of a dedicated TriPylon wallet.

01 / OPERATING SYSTEM

Nucleo OS onboard

The same modular wallet operating-system architecture used across TriPylon devices.

02 / KEY HANDLING

Keys stay offline

The device keeps sensitive key operations separate from the connected host.

03 / HARDWARE SAFEGUARDS

Protected foundation

First-generation Touch combines signed-firmware startup, encrypted storage and locked debug access.
